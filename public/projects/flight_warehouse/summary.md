# Flight Warehouse

The goal was to provide templates and documentation that let anyone spin up HPC environments in a private cloud, from admin-built team clusters down to personal single-node environments.

# What was done

We built Flight Warehouse around Flight Solo, offering ready-made cloud templates for the common cluster types each with step-by-step launch, administration and usage guidance. The templates showed how a single packaged image could be tailored into different environments with cloud-init and the Flight tools, and surfaced the key considerations of workload lifetime, infrastructure costs and collaboration requirements.

# What was learnt

We learnt how to package HPC environments as repeatable cloud templates without maintaining an image per variant, how ephemeral and long-lived environments need different defaults around persistence, backups and scheduling, and how much guidance a self-service HPC offering needs to be genuinely usable and cost-aware for both administrators and non-technical users.
